## ⚖️ Law Firms (Chicago Focus)  
- **Leakage Problem:**  
  - Most law firms lose **7–9 potential clients weekly** due to poor visibility, intake delays, or weak follow-up.  
  - This translates to **$10K–$20K per month** in missed revenue.  

- **Proof Points:**  
  - Google Local Service Ads dominate the top 3 spots. Firms not there lose 60%+ of clicks.  
  - Intake response delay > 15 minutes = 80% lost leads.  

- **Competitor Trends:**  
  - Aggressive SEO and PPC spend.  
  - Firms buying leads from aggregators like Avvo, Thumbtack.  

- **Hot Buttons:**  
  - Missed cases = lost revenue.  
  - Intake gap is silent profit drain.  
  - “Every day you delay, those clients go elsewhere.”

## 📅 Closing / Booking Tactics  
- Always offer **two specific time slots**.  
- Scripts:  
  - “Let’s carve out 15 minutes this week. Tuesday at 2 or Wednesday at 10?”  
  - “We’ll keep it short, but those lost clients won’t wait another week.”  

- Never say: “When are you free?” → Too open-ended.  
- Always anchor with urgency + specificity.
